Pronounce

On-device pronunciation feedback for English language learners.

The Pronounce desktop app is being built for Pace University's International Academic Support (IAS) office. The pilot installer will be available here soon.

How it will work

When the app is ready you'll download and install it from this page. On first launch it sets up its on-device speech model automatically — your audio stays on your computer and never leaves your device.

The speech model that powers the app is hosted on this site and fetched by the app automatically; it is not a manual download.
